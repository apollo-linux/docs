Hi, all!

We now have an initial set of Live ISO files that users can test. These are in a very early state, and do not ship with a functional build of our installer program, so you'll need to [use bootc install](https://gist.github.com/fizzyizzy05/c591efd7c97447ee4f907dfab417d2a3) to do a manual installation for the time being. When the time comes, we'll do a bigger call for testing for the installer ahead of the Alpha release. This is one of the last major steps before the Alpha release, the final thing we're waiting to complete is the installer.

These ISOs are available through a [GitHub Actions artefact](https://github.com/apollo-linux/apollo/actions/workflows/build-iso.yml), you will need to be signed into GitHub to download these for now. In the future we intend to move to a proper storage setup long-term. 

Please try these out and give feedback by filing issues on GitHub. You can also keep an eye on our [ISO Tracking issue](https://github.com/apollo-linux/apollo/actions/workflows/build-iso.yml). 

Thanks!
